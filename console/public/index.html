      Morse code is a single tone keyed on and off, and hams still use it because it gets through when voice cannot. This reads it for you and writes out the letters. To find some, switch to CW and tune to a thin line that flickers on the waterfall. Or stay in FM on a repeater: every ten minutes or so it sends its call sign in Morse.
